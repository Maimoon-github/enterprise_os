# Platform Character & Formatting Limits

- **LinkedIn:** Max 3,000 characters (First 210 chars above fold).
- **YouTube:** Title max 100 characters; Description max 5,000 characters.
- **Instagram:** Max 2,200 characters (First 125 chars above fold). Max 30 hashtags.
- **Threads:** Max 500 characters.
- **Facebook:** Recommended 80 characters for feed posts (max 63,206).
- **X (Twitter):** Max 280 characters standard.
- **Pinterest:** Pin title max 100 characters; Pin description max 500 characters.
- **Google Business Profile:** Post max 1,500 characters (Recommended 150-300).
