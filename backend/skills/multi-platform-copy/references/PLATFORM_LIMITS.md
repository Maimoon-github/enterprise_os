# Platform Character & Formatting Limits Reference

Comprehensive limits and hook conventions across all 8 supported social distribution platforms, aligned with `app.agents.validators.platform_limits`.

| Platform | Hard Limit | Above-the-Fold Truncation | Recommended Hashtags | Media Constraints |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LinkedIn** | 3,000 chars | 210 chars | 3–5 targeted tags | 1:1, 16:9, 4:5 image/video |
| **YouTube** | Title: 100 chars<br>Desc: 5,000 chars | Title: 70 chars | 3 primary tags | 16:9 landscape video, 9:16 Shorts ($\le 60\text{s}$) |
| **Instagram** | 2,200 chars | 125 chars | 5–15 (max 30) | 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 Reels/Stories |
| **Threads** | 500 chars | Full view | 1–3 inline | 1:1, 9:16 media attachments |
| **Facebook** | 63,206 chars | 400 chars (rec: 80) | 0–2 tags | 1:1, 16:9, 9:16 reels/posts |
| **X (Twitter)** | 280 chars | Full view | 1–2 tags max | 16:9, 1:1 image/video |
| **Pinterest** | Title: 100 chars<br>Desc: 500 chars | 50 chars in feed | 2–5 search terms | 2:3 vertical pins, 1:1 square |
| **Google Business Profile** | 1,500 chars | 150–300 chars | None (SEO keywords) | 1:1 square, 16:9, 4:5 |

## Quality & Formatting Rules
1. **Zero Raw URLs in Fold:** Place destination hyperlinks either in designated call-to-action buttons or post-fold.
2. **Whitespace Hierarchy:** Separate positioning hooks from body points with single linebreaks to maintain readability.
3. **Emoji Restraint:** Use $\le 3$ relevant emojis per post to retain authoritative enterprise B2B tone.
